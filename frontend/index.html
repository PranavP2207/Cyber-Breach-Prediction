Breach rate by kill chain stage reached

The single strongest signal in the data: the further an attacker gets before being stopped, the more likely the intrusion ends in a breach.

What else drives the prediction

  • Lateral movement, privilege escalation and persistence are the three strongest individual signals of an eventual breach, ahead of any single defensive control.
  • MFA is the most effective control in the dataset — enforcing it cuts the raw breach rate from 37.3% to 26.8%.
  • Detection time matters more than response time: slower detection correlates more strongly with breach success than slower containment once detected.

Pipeline

↓

↓

Feature engineering
One-hot encode 2 categorical fields (Attack_Stage, Company_Size) · pass through 14 numeric fields → 16 model features
↓
04 · SPLIT
Train / test split
80,000 train / 20,000 test, stratified on Attack_Success
↓
05 · TRAIN

Logistic Regression baseline · Random Forest (150 trees, max depth 12)
↓
06 · EVALUATE
